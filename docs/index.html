Typoscope

for KOReader

A reading mask that keeps one line in view. Like placing two pieces of paper over a printed page, Typoscope covers the text above and below the line you’re reading.

Install the plugin Source on GitHub

Installation

  1. Download and extract the install ZIP from the latest release.
  2. Copy the plugin folder into KOReader’s plugins directory. Name the folder typoscope.koplugin.
  3. Restart KOReader and open a book. In the menu, choose Tools → More tools → Typoscope reading mask → Enable mask.

koreader/plugins/typoscope.koplugin/

Depending on your KOReader version, Typoscope may appear directly under Tools. Before reading, assign Toggle typoscope to an easy-to-remember gesture so you can quickly turn the mask off.

While you read

Tap in your usual page-turn zones to step forward or backward through the lines. At the end of a page, another tap turns the page or scrolls the view.

In Mask color, choose light or dark. You can also adjust line padding, change the cleaning flash on each step, and leave pages containing images uncovered.

The mask works with reflowable books, such as EPUBs, in page and scroll modes. PDF and DjVu documents aren’t supported. The book’s text and colors stay as they are.
